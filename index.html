Who are we?

ISBAT University is a hub for practical skills and career development. We bridge the gap between classroom theory and real-world industry demands through expert-led courses, hands-on workshops, and flexible learning paths.

Whether you are upgrading your technical expertise or starting a new career, our certified instructors and modern facilities give you the tools to succeed.

Courses we offer

Four hands-on programs, taught by working professionals.

  • Web Development

    Build modern, responsive, high-performance websites.

  • Computer Networking

  • Database Management

    Design, organise, and secure large-scale data systems.

  • Cyber Security

    Learn the technical skills and analytical tools to defend against online threats.
